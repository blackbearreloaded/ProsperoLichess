# Pictures at the first frame, while the title arrives, when it is complete,
# and of the home screen that follows. Storage of its own, emptied first (the
# title is shorter under reduced motion, which an earlier run may have set).
guest
fresh
wait 1
mark title-first
wait 24
mark title-arriving
wait 70
mark title-complete
wait 240
mark home-arriving
wait 240
mark home
wait 120
mark end
wait 300
quit
