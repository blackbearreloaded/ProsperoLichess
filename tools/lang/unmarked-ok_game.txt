# Values the game screens put on screen and do not translate, for
# "tools/strings.py unmarked": one regular expression a line.

# The board's coordinates: the files a to h (the ranks are digits).
^[a-h]$
# The first letter of a player's name, on the portrait beside it.
^[A-Z]$
# A name in capitals: the opponent on the card of an offer, Stockfish on the
# waiting screen's ticket.
\b(?:NORDICKNIGHT|BLACKBEAR|STOCKFISH)\b
# What the glyphs of the shoulder buttons say, in the hint row.
^[LR][123]$
